Disease - Cinematic

Disease - Unreal Engine cinematic

In this project, I started by gathering references and creating a moodboard. Next, I edited the music in Adobe Premiere Pro and blocked out the entire sequence. From there, I handled the level art, lighting, animation, and character lip-syncing, before moving on to the final polish. Everything was created within Unreal Engine 5.
